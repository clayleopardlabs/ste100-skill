# STE Section 3: Verbs (Rules 3.1–3.7)

| Rule | Summary |
|------|---------|
| **3.1** | Use only verb tenses from the approved list: imperative, simple present, simple past, past participle (as adjective), simple future |
| **3.2** | Do not use: present perfect, past perfect, present/past progressive, or other complex forms |
| **3.3** | Use past participle as an adjective (before noun or after "to be"/"to become") — this is NOT passive voice |
| **3.4** | Do not use helping verbs to make complex verb structures (e.g., "has adjusted", "is adjusted", "will be adjusted") |
| **3.5** | Use "-ing" form only as a technical name or modifier in a technical name. The only approved "-ing" words are: lighting, opening, routing, servicing (nouns); mating, missing, remaining (adjectives); something (pronoun); during (preposition) |
| **3.6** | Use ONLY active voice in procedural writing. Use active voice as much as possible in descriptive writing |
| **3.7** | Write one action per sentence. Each sentence has ONE verb that describes ONE action |

## Deep Dive

**Approved tenses (Rule 3.1):** imperative, simple present, simple past, simple future, and past participle used as an adjective. That is all.

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| "Remove the screw." (imperative) | "The screw must be removed." (passive + must) |
| "The system operates at 400 Hz." (simple present) | "The system has operated since 2005." (present perfect) |
| "The pilot adjusted the trim." (simple past) | "The pilot was adjusting the trim." (past progressive) |
| "The switch will close the valve." (simple future) | "The valve will be closed by the switch." (future passive) |
| "The closed valve..." (past participle as adjective) | "The valve is closed by the motor." (passive voice) |

**Rule 3.3 vs passive voice — the trap:** "The valve is closed" is ONLY acceptable as a state ("to be" + past participle = adjective describing a condition, e.g. "The valve is closed. Do not open it."). It is passive voice when it describes an action by an agent ("The valve is closed by the motor."). When in doubt, restate in active voice: "The motor closes the valve."

**Rule 3.5 — the -ing check:** A word ending in "-ing" is almost always a violation unless it is one of the approved -ing words (lighting, opening, routing, servicing, mating, missing, remaining, something, during) or part of a technical name ("the operating room", "the landing gear"). The linter flags these as WARNINGs — decide each one deliberately.

**Rule 3.7 — one action per sentence:** "Remove the cover and disconnect the cable." -> two sentences. Split: "Remove the cover." / "Disconnect the cable."

**How to fix passive voice:** find the actor, make it the subject, use the plain verb.

- "The filter is replaced by the operator." -> "The operator replaces the filter."
- "The test was performed." -> "Do the test."
